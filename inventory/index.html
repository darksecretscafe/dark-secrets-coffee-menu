Owner setup — start here

The owner can enter actual stock items and recipes later. No ingredients, packaging quantities, purchase costs, or recipe measurements are prefilled. Follow these steps in the same integrated preview tab so temporary records can be used across modules.

1. Register stock items

Create ingredients and packaging with the correct base unit (g, ml, pcs). Set minimum stock and supplier if known.

Open Stock Master
2. Receive purchases

Record purchase quantities and total costs when actual supplier invoices are available. Convert kg/l to g/ml automatically.

Open Receiving
3. Enter recipes

Choose each drink and add measured ingredient/packaging quantities. Leave recipes empty until measurements are confirmed.

Open Recipes
4. Review costing

Check weighted-average unit costs. Missing costs are shown as incomplete rather than guessed.

Open Costing

Important: Links open the same integrated preview page, but refreshing or opening another tab starts a new empty demo. This is not persistent owner data entry yet. Order integration and stock deduction remain disabled.
