Stock Adjustments & Wastage

Use positive or negative adjustments to correct stock counts. Wastage always reduces stock. Every demo entry is appended to the activity history; no deletion or editing of past movements.

Weighted Average Cost (WAC)

Calculated from demo receipts, plus adjustments and wastage. Opening stock and positive adjustments without a known cost remain unvalued; they must be costed before production valuation. Negative movements reduce value at the current average cost, not at the newest receipt cost.

Inventory itemBalanceAverage cost / base unitInventory valueCost status

Example: 1 kg at ₱600 + 1 kg at ₱800 → 2,000 g at ₱0.70/g. Wastage of 100 g reduces inventory value by ₱70 while keeping ₱0.70/g WAC. All calculations are preview-only and subject to production precision/concurrency validation.
